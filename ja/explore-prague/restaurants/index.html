レストラン

Hungry? Here are our favourite places to eat, from the riverside right next to the dock to the old Czech pubs in the centre.

Walking times are from the houseboats and are approximate.

NEAR THE DOCK

The Italians

Bakery, bar, restaurant, excellent quality, about 25% more expensive than the average. Just across the street and 200m towards prague center. This is our default recommendation!

U Peronu

Typical Czech, cross the street and near the gas station. Great beer, Czech modern dishes.

Huong Viet

Vietnamese food, delicious, but probably a takeaway option. Just behind The Italians on the tram street. You can order in advance by phone and they ask for 10 to 15 minutes.

These are our favourites, but there is easily 10 more cafes and restaurants less than 3 minutes walking from the houseboats.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Reserve ahead on Friday and Saturday evenings, especially in the old town pubs.
  • Tipping: not obligatory, but around 10% is normal for good service. Tell the waiter the total you want to pay. Check first that service is not already on the bill.
  • Lunch menu (polední menu): many places serve a cheap daily lunch menu on weekdays, usually from 11:00.
  • Christmas Eve, 24 December: many restaurants CLOSE or serve only a set menu. U Fleků is closed. Book early! See also "Christmas Time in Prague".
  • Cards are accepted almost everywhere, but keep some cash (CZK) for small places and markets.
