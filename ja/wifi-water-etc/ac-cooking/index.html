エアコン・料理

A/C, Heating, Cooking

Infrared heating panels

White boards installed on the wall in some houseboats.

The units are mainly installed in the sleeping area on the upper floor.

Switch it on approx 2 hours before going to sleep. Don't leave them on all day. Save energy.

A/C units (heating & cooling function)

The remote control has a "MODE" button.

To heat the room, press MODE  until you see the HEAT sign or a SUN icon. It might take several minutes for the unit to start heating.

To cool the room, press MODE  until you see the COOL sign or a SNOW icon. It might take several minutes for the unit to start heating.

The other modes are not usually used.

Sleep icon is useful for night time when it goes to the quiet functioning.

The remote icons for A/C can differ a bit.

Floor Heating

No intervention needed. It is automatic
