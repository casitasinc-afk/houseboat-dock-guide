Our story

I am Alex. I was born in Uruguay but have been in Czechia since 1995. I grew up next to the sea in Montevideo. I found Prague's marvelous river gives me a lot of the joys of the sea. I have lived in my boat for the last 10 years. I have 4 wonderful kids. I studied Mechanical Engineering and Economics & Management.

You will see me working or better barbecuing on the terrace of Klara. I love playing the guitar, writing songs and I welcome every hello and visit from guests and friends alike. I speak fluent English, Czech, Spanish and mumble my way through in some more languages.

The last 10 years have been an exciting ride, hopefully offering memorable stays in this amazing city.

With the years my team caught on to help me build what you see. Count on us to make it memorable!

Thank you for coming and hope we deserve a new visit in the future.

The Houseboat Dock team
