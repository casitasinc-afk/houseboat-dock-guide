Heating & Cooling

The remote control has a "MODE" button.

To heat the room, press MODE  until you see the HEAT sign or a SUN icon. It might take several minutes for the unit to start heating.

To cool the room, press MODE  until you see the COOL sign or a SNOW icon. It might take several minutes for the unit to start heating.

The other modes are not usually used.

The remote icons for A/C can differ a bit
