ARRIVE & PARK

Free parking strictly requires these steps:

  1. Send us the CAR PLATE NUMBERS  (eg. ABA3235) without hyphen, via Airbnb chat . Do this ideally right now. This will increase your chance of parking close to the houseboats.
  2. Watch THIS VIDEO with parking options 🙂
  3. Tap parking A, B, or C to see parking options.

Parking  A.  80m from the houseboats. Use spots WITHOUT company logos.

Parking B  60m from houseboats. Spots against the concrete wall.

Parking C (closest 8 spots) 50m from houseboats. Keep the entire car behind the white line!

Then walk to the houseboats walking through parking C.

IMPORTANT: Parking spots are limited to 12 cars. It might be that ALL spaces are taken and the parking has to be done outside the port. This is relatively rare.

Exit the port in the same, north direction. The exit gate opens automatically.

IMPORTANT!

Once arrived, send us a photo of the parked cars with plate numbers through Airbnb chat. Parking in the wrong spot is €10/hour, tow truck fee is €250! PLEASE AVOID PROBLEMS

CHECK-IN

Check your houseboat name & lockbox code from the Airbnb arrival guide. Walk to this spot. Locate your houseboat in this map(location of houseboats). Open the lockbox and enter. Close the lockbox and shuffle the numbers. Drop your baggage and take a minute to check the houseboat

Report immediately any damage or problem.

WALKING TO THE PORT

Use this blue gate (Google maps location). Lockbox code 3570 to enter. Put the key back, close the lockbox and shuffle the numbers. Always lock the door, after entering or exiting.

Read  "Taxi, Metro, Tram, Bus"
