游泳

游泳

Warnings:

Officially swimming is forbidden.

Even though some people swim in the river, the water might cause some allergy reaction in a small percentage of people. Do not swim, and if you decide to swim, do it not beyond 20m from the coast, where the waterway starts. You are at risk of being run over and drowned by boat traffic!

Please be VERY careful as you might not be able to return to the boat and climb aboard safely unless you are fit. Some houseboats have ladders to climb back. Wear a buoy to become more visible by boats.

We discourage swimming. You swim in the river at your own risk.

Swimming in any flood condition of the river is strictly forbidden and enforced by police.
