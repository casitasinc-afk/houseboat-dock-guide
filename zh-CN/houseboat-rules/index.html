船屋规则

NO NOISE AFTER 10 PM !!

SENSITIVE TOILET!

Nothing can be thrown in except toilet paper provided by us. Repair costs €1000 !

SMOKING

No smoking inside the boat. No cigarette butts to the river or garbage. Smoking outside? Ask neighbors and keep doors and windows closed.

HEATING & COOLING

Check Airbnb details on specific houseboat. A/C  remote:  Sun icon  heats, snow icon cools. Temp 24 is good. Sleep icon is quiet mode.

WHEN LEAVING THE HOUSEBOAT TO THE CITY

Put what can be blown away or get wet inside. Close doors and windows or in vent if available.
