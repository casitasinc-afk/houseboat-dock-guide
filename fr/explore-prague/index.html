Découvrir Prague

Découvrir Prague

Getting into the city:

Walking 10min to the Railway Bridge, then cross it, and you are already entering the older parts of Prague town - Vysehrad and the river bank.

Use station "Smichovske Nadrazi" to take: Metro or Tram, 10 minutes to the city (Mustek)

Family-Friendly Itinerary for Prague with or without kids:

1. Prague Castle & St. Vitus Cathedral – Explore the castle, watch the changing of the guard, and enjoy city views.

2. Charles Bridge & Old Town Square – Walk across the bridge, see the Astronomical Clock, and enjoy street performers.

3. Prague Zoo – Spend a few hours seeing animals and enjoying interactive exhibits.

4. Boat Ride on the Vltava River with our captain – Short 45 min cruise for relaxing sightseeing from the water. Price 15 EUR per person

5. Petrin Hill & Observation Tower – Climb the tower, explore the mirror maze, and enjoy the park.

6. (Optional, if time allows) National Technical Museum or Lego Museum – Hands-on fun for curious minds.
