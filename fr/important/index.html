Important

Extreme weather, fire, etc.

The person who made the reservation is responsible for the ship in all weather and technical conditions.

Fire extinguishers are located on the dock and in most houseboats.

Please follow the weather forecast online. If you see forecasts of rain, high winds, please take measures in advance :

  • Leave windows closed or in vent position if going to town
  • Store  terrace cushions, glasses, plates, all inside the houseboat

In case of blackout, do not use the toilet or water or shower. If the blackout is prolonged use the gas station MOL.
