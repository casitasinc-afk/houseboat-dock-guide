Coupure de courant – premiers secours

Blackouts in the houseboats are more common than in an apartment due to capacity reasons and more requirements due to weather especially in very warm or very cold days.

If you are able to act on an electricity board, check the panels like on this picture. IMPORTANT: ALWAYS WEAR RUBBER SOLE SHOES when touching any electrical panel or appliance!

See below and contact us in case of need!

All switches should be up. (unless there is a sign, tape, or anything unusual).

Electrical panels are on the dock, similar to this one. Some are back to back.

Check these for up position, near the blue gate.

These can also be tripped down.

At the beginning of the parking lot, check if number 9 has all interruptors up (

No. 9 inside (you need a key or something that rotates the square peg)

Further away, number 8 has a few switches that also must be up.

Check these inside. Always check nobody is doing work downstream!

IMPORTANT: PLEASE REPORT WHICH SWITCH WAS DOWN. TAKE A PICTURE BEFORE AND AFTER.
